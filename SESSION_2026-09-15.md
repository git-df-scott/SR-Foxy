|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

coverage of the saturated set; they are not progressively wider searches, and a

## Operational rules learned the expensive way

* **Container lifetime is about 26 h.** Four Teichner runs with 24 h budgets
  died with the container having completed zero partner searches. Budget 20 h
  or less, and prefer a box that finishes to one that is merely larger.
* **Teichner cost is superlinear in the sum's crossing number**: 4.21 h at 31,
  7.96 h at 33, over 12 h at 35 for both 35-crossing partners tried.
* **Per-diagram cost varies by 13x inside one box at one crossing number**
  (1685.9 s to 21582.1 s across six 27-crossing K_B diagrams). Budget from
  measurement, never from crossing number.
* **Write incrementally and keep a heartbeat.** Two 5 h kills on the
  pre-heartbeat build left nothing at all; every later kill left a usable
  record. `results/teichner_killed_runs_archive.json` holds all 14 runs.

`D_{0,1}` does not shrink: 25 crossings over 120 seeds, and `K_1` 19 over 400.

**[16 Sep] WITHDRAWN — see `ERRATA_2026-09-16.md` (E16-1).** Miyazaki Thm 5.5
requires *every* prime fibered summand to be minimal in the homotopy-ribbon
order or to have no nonunit norm factor of `Delta`. A nontrivial ribbon `J`
fails **both**: it is above the unknot in the ribbon order, and Fox-Milnor makes
`Delta_J` itself a nonunit norm. So the theorem does not apply to `D_{0,1} # J`
and cannot obstruct any Teichner sum. `8_9`, `8_20`, `9_27` are legitimate
partners at 33, 33 and 34 crossings, and are running
(`results/teichner_D01_fibered_partners.json`). The correct rule is that the sum
is excluded iff **every prime summand of `J`** satisfies an alternative — which
rules out `J = L # (-L)`, e.g. the square knot, and never rules out a prime
fibered ribbon `J`.

That leaves `9_41`/`9_46` at 34 crossings and `10_3`/`10_22`/`10_87` at 35,
after which the non-fibered ribbon knots of at most 10 crossings are exhausted.

**The lane runs out of tractable partners rather than failing.** Given section
1, the right move when it does is not 11-crossing partners but the untouched
dials on `6_1`, which is what is running now.

## The strategic picture

`research/24` argues that every route-B certificate reduces to the same missing
object, a pair of distinct concordant fibered knots, and that satellite
constructions cannot supply one because satelliting a ribbon concordance yields
a ribbon concordance. Teichner is the only constructive lane that escapes this.
It also records one untried crossing: an r = 0 pair whose partner already
carries a non-ribbon certificate would be a counterexample outright, needing no
concordance coincidence. That cannot be run here (all ten stored MP exteriors
are hyperbolic, and the RBG generator lived outside this clone) but it is cheap
wherever those files are.
