# Abandoned weapons, 2010–2026

This is a literature-archaeology report, not a list of names. Historical leads came from [D01]; theorem claims below are tied to primary sources. “No application found” is a documented search result, not a proof of nonexistence.

## 1. Friedl-style metabelian and twisted-Alexander techniques

**Original mechanism.** Homotopy-ribbon concordance induces strong algebraic restrictions on Alexander modules, Blanchfield pairings and suitable twisted Alexander polynomials; see [S19]. A ribbon disk is homotopy-ribbon, so violation obstructs ribbonness.

**Was it applied to GST?** No candidate-specific GST computation was found in the primary GST follow-up chain. The historical Friedl comment located through [D01] already observed the conceptual problem: GST’s displayed disk exterior has the required π₁-surjectivity shape. The 2026 DG knot is stronger still—proved handle-ribbon [S01].

**Verdict.** For `DG` and fixed GST band sums with the exhibited handle disk, this is **proved structurally unable to obstruct** at the homotopy-ribbon level, not merely computationally neglected. More elaborate representations could still encode ribbon-only information, but no such theorem was verified. Modern software can accelerate representation enumeration; it cannot overcome a theorem whose necessary condition is already satisfied.

## 2. Cappell–Shaneson / irregular dihedral covers

**Original and modern theorem.** The Cappell–Shaneson branched-cover program is made explicit by the Ξ invariant and signature bounds in [S18, Thm. 3]. The 2026 extension criterion [S13, Thm. 1.1] reduces extension of a dihedral quotient to a congruence on a characteristic knot; [S13, Thm. 7.1] states the corresponding (homotopy-)ribbon bound.

**Was it applied to GST?** The idea was suggested in the 2010 discussion [D01]. No completed primary GST calculation was found. This distinction matters, but it does not rescue the flagship use: GST and `DG` are already handle-ribbon, hence homotopy-ribbon. Any valid coloring obstruction to existence of *some* homotopy-ribbon disk must vanish or fail to supply the required universal quantifier.

**Verdict.** Historically **never carried through** on GST; today **conceptually killed for proving non-ribbonness of an already handle-ribbon object**. It remains computationally viable on candidates whose homotopy-ribbon status is open. The 2026 congruence criterion removes much of the old computation barrier but not the logical one.

## 3. Turaev’s multiplace Seifert forms

**Original theorem.** Turaev [S20] constructs forms πⁿ→ℤ using integral representatives of Milnor residues. The primary abstract states that the π³ form can obstruct ribbonness even where the ordinary Seifert form does not. The 2010 discussion points to “Theorem H,” but an open primary text sufficient to transcribe its exact hypotheses was not recovered.

**Was it applied to GST?** No published candidate calculation was found. Unlike the preceding two weapons, the available description is ribbon-specific rather than explicitly only homotopy-ribbon. It therefore cannot be declared structurally dead merely because the targets are handle-ribbon.

**Why abandoned?** Evidence supports “never really carried through,” not “proved useless.” The practical obstacles are: reconstructing a usable π³ presentation from a high-genus/high-crossing surface; controlling basing and Milnor-residue choices; and translating Theorem H into a reproducible finite certificate. The first required task is textual, not computational: obtain and verify the complete theorem statement.

**Modern prospects.** Group-computation and exact nilpotent-quotient packages plausibly remove much of the arithmetic burden once the theorem is formalized. This is a legitimate archaeology lead, but its present evidence level is too low to call an active obstruction.

## 4. Deeper derived-series / solvable / polycyclic refinements

**Original suggestion.** The 2010 discussion proposed passing beyond metabelian quotients. Later work such as [S19] gives rigorous higher-order restrictions in homotopy-ribbon concordance settings.

**GST application.** No primary paper applying a polycyclic tower to a fixed GST band sum was found. More importantly, a refinement whose conclusion is only “not homotopy-ribbon” cannot succeed against a knot with an exhibited handle-ribbon disk.

**Verdict.** The generic proposal was **never carried through**, but the basic attack is **conceptually blocked** on `DG`/GST. A live version needs a new theorem about the kernel normally generated by ribbon-disk minima, or another property distinguishing a 0/1-handle disk exterior from a 0/1/2-handle exterior. No such polycyclic ribbon-only theorem was verified.

## 5. Derivative-link refinements

**Theorem.** Park–Powell [S17] obstruct ℚ[ℤ]-homology ribbonness and constrain triple Milnor linking numbers of derivatives for homotopy-ribbon/doubly-slice knots. Miller–Zupan [S02, Prop. 1.1 and Thm. 1.3] provide the sharper geometric dichotomy needed here: unlink derivative iff ribbon, R-link derivative iff handle-ribbon.

**Applications.** No Park–Powell computation was found for `DG` or GST. `DG` has an explicit R-link derivative [S01], but the global set of derivatives has not been classified.

**Verdict.** **Not carried through and still logically live**, provided the calculation targets the unlink-derivative requirement rather than merely reproving failure of a chosen derivative. A single bad derivative never obstructs ribbonness; every possible Seifert surface/derivative relevant to Proposition 1.1 must be controlled.

## Archaeology conclusion

| weapon | historical GST computation | 2026 status on DG/GST | reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| metabelian/twisted Alexander | none found | proved useless at homotopy-ribbon level | targets already handle-ribbon |
| irregular dihedral covers | suggested, none found | proved useless at homotopy-ribbon level | targets already handle-ribbon |
| Turaev π³ form | none found | genuinely untested / theorem transcription incomplete | may be ribbon-specific |
| polycyclic/derived towers | none found | generic form conceptually blocked | needs a ribbon-only necessity |
| derivative triple linking / unlink derivatives | none found | untested and potentially live | directly engages ribbon versus handle-ribbon |

The useful abandoned-weapon lesson is negative: do not spend a campaign recomputing sophisticated obstructions to a weaker property the candidate is already proved to have.
