# Paired clasps can change nonabelian labels without changing cyclic linking

18 September 2026. A local geometric derivation and a conditional first-order
construction, independently audited. No marked global annulus has been built.

## The question and the useful distinction

After research/42, can we vary the embedded boundary axes while retaining their
based group words, auxiliary unlink type, and exact equivariant linking matrix
E, in order to simplify an immersed annulus in a specified disk exterior?

There is a local operation with precisely this purpose. Opposite clasp doubles
of two based companion curves of the same meridional exponent have cancelling
cyclic self-linking contributions but different full-group double-point labels.
The resulting construction cancels the kernel of the cyclic projection of the
**primary invariant of a fixed annulus**, under the hypotheses below. This is
our derivation, not a theorem attributed to the cited paper. An independent
agent audit checked the local calculation and algebra; that is not a substitute
for an explicit diagram and annulus movie in the present application.

## Setup and exact hypotheses

Let M be the exterior of R in S3, with meridional exponent e: pi1(M) -> Z.
Let a,b be the marked auxiliary unlink, each of exponent zero. Use preferred
zero framings, fixed whiskers, and compatible lifts for E. Assume:

1. A specified smooth slice disk D for R gives W = B4 minus an open tubular
   neighborhood of D. The actual boundary inclusion q: pi1(M) -> pi1(W) is
   surjective and preserves the specified exponent maps. Surjectivity is a
   hypothesis here, not an assertion for arbitrary slice disks.
2. A particular properly immersed annulus A in W, with specified basing and
   boundary framings, has one boundary component b. Its core image is gamma,
   with e(gamma)=0. The other boundary component is left fixed.
3. We may replace b by the band insertions described below, and append their
   traces in a boundary collar. Those traces are stationary at its marked point.

The saved algebraic q0 is not yet certified as this geometric q. Nor is the
annulus A or its invariant currently available. The statement is conditional
on supplying them; a group conjugacy is not a recorded annulus movie.

## The local clasp calculation

For a based word g in pi1(M), choose a zero-framed companion unknot c_g in M,
with a band to b, representing g using that band as whisker. Its configuration
with a,b is required to be the image of a standard split configuration under
an ambient isotopy of S3 fixing a,b, while crossings through R are allowed
during the isotopy. This requirement retains the underlying unlink and band
type. It is realizable by meridional disk pushes: the knot group is normally
generated by meridians, and the pushing arcs can avoid the other one-dimensional
components. The final companion tubes and bands avoid R and each other.

Inside the companion solid torus insert a genuine untwisted clasp double k_g,
with clasp sign epsilon. The following properties must be kept separate:

- Its winding number in that solid torus is zero. Hence it is null-homotopic
  there, and band insertion leaves the based word of b unchanged.
- The untwisted clasp double of the zero-framed unknot is an unknot. Transport
  of the entire standard companion-and-band configuration therefore preserves
  the auxiliary unlink after forgetting R. Arbitrary knotted bands would not
  justify this assertion.
- A disk **map** bounded by k_g lies inside its companion tube. Its lift to the
  cyclic cover is compact and avoids all other component lifts. Consequently
  every mixed equivariant linking entry with the old axes or another disjoint
  companion tube is exactly zero. A generic such 3D map has double curves;
  the isolated double point used next belongs to a four-dimensional trace.

Put n=e(g). The standard clasp-change trace has one double point labelled g.
In its cyclic cover the off-diagonal intersections with a normal push-off
contribute at deck exponents n and -n. The preferred zero-framing correction
is minus twice that sign at exponent zero. Thus, for one fixed global
boundary/intersection convention sigma in {+1,-1},

    E(k_g, k_g^+) = sigma * epsilon * (t^n + t^(-n) - 2).       (1)

There is no extra factor two outside this expression. At t=1 it is zero, as
ordinary zero framing requires; at n=0 the whole expression vanishes. The
companion's transported zero framing is essential: twisting the pattern is
not covered by (1). This derivation uses the signed intersections of the
standard local clasp trace, not a numerical linking estimate.

The pair-of-pants band cobordism has exponent-zero fundamental group because
its input boundary components b and k_g do. It has a compact lift. With fixed
zero-framed input collars and the mixed terms already zero, the bounding-chain
argument of research/42 gives

    E(b band-sum k_g) = E(b) + E(k_g).

For two disjoint transported companion tubes, the same argument applies to
both insertions. If e(g)=e(h), choose opposite clasp signs. Equation (1) shows
their self contributions cancel exactly. All mixed entries remain unchanged.
Each insertion leaves the based word unchanged, and the transported standard
configuration preserves the auxiliary unlink.

## What changes in the four-dimensional invariant

Use the additive module for the chosen annulus, before taking any orbit space:

    Q_gamma = Z[ <gamma> \ pi1(W) / <gamma> ] /
              ( [x]=[x^-1], [1]=0 ).

The double-point loop of the local trace maps to q(g). With the trace stationary
at the marked point, its latitude is 1, so composition adds its contribution
directly. Paired opposite clasps therefore change the reduced primary invariant
by

    +/- ( [q(g)] - [q(h)] ).                                (2)

If the basing moves, the composition conjugation must instead be recorded.
For a single exponent-zero insertion, the change is +/-[q(g)] and (1) is zero.
A single clasp trace need not be relatively framed. An oppositely signed local
kink, whose double-point label is 1, restores its relative normal framing;
the reduced module discards that label. Paired opposite clasps have cancelling
normal-Euler contributions. Neither procedure constructs clean Whitney disks.

Schneiderman's *Algebraic linking numbers of knots in 3-manifolds*, AGT 3
(2003), 921--968, DOI [10.2140/agt.2003.3.921](https://doi.org/10.2140/agt.2003.3.921),
[Sections 3.2 and 4.1](https://msp.org/agt/2003/3-2/agt-v3-n2-p10-p.pdf),
provides the clasp-labelled construction and the annulus double-coset target.
Its global relative invariants concern products of 3-manifolds with an interval;
we use the local trace and the invariant of a fixed annulus, not a claimed
classification theorem for W. Primary source checked 18 September 2026.

## The exact algebraic consequence

Because e(gamma)=0, exponent descends to double cosets. There is a projection

    p: Q_gamma -> Z[t,t^-1] / (t^n=t^-n, 1=0).

The target is free abelian on positive absolute exponents. The source is free
abelian on the nonidentity inversion-orbits of double cosets. It follows
directly, by grouping coefficients by absolute exponent, that ker(p) is
generated by:

- classes with exponent zero;
- differences of two classes with the same absolute exponent.

Invert a representative when necessary to make the signed exponents equal.
Surjectivity and exponent compatibility of q then provide boundary words g,h
with those exponents. A finite decomposition of any element of ker(p) can
therefore be realized by finitely many single zero-exponent clasps and paired
equal-exponent clasps as above.

**Conditional conclusion:** if p(mu(A))=0 for the specified immersed annulus,
its endpoint may be modified to cancel this reduced primary invariant while
preserving the based word, auxiliary unlink, and exact E. This changes the
embedded endpoint. It does not prove concordance for the original endpoint,
and does not identify a subsequent surgery knot with an Abe--Tagami knot.

The subsequent argument in research/47 supplies p(mu(A))=0 when A is an actual
exponent-zero annulus in an actual slice-disk exterior and its boundary matrix
E is exactly zero. No explicit A has yet been built for the current marked
data. Higher Whitney-disk intersections and twisting remain after primary
cancellation.

## A separate obstruction to the general embedding shortcuts

For any smooth properly embedded disk D in B4, Mayer--Vietoris for its tubular
neighborhood and exterior, with overlap D2 x S1, gives H2(W;Z)=0. Consequently
the ordinary intersection of any closed immersed sphere in W with any proper
oriented annulus is zero: it factors through

    H2(W;Z) x H2(W, boundary W;Z) -> Z.

Augmentation of the unreduced equivariant sphere/annulus pairing is this
ordinary intersection. A pairing equal to 1, or any single group element,
would augment to 1 and is impossible. Thus there is **no algebraically dual
sphere of that kind in the unmodified disk exterior**. This strengthens
research/43's unchecked-dual warning to an actual obstruction. Stabilizing the
ambient manifold can change this situation, but would no longer solve the
required standard-B4 problem without a separate destabilization argument.
