# The next annulus calculation must retain nonabelian labels

18 September 2026. Source audit after the construction in research/42.

## Exact question

Assume the saved q0 has been identified with inclusion into a specified smooth
disk exterior W. Our modified axes then have conjugate images. Can a properly
immersed annulus realizing that conjugacy be replaced by a smooth embedded
annulus with the required framing? Neither E=0 nor the genus-13 boundary
surface answers this question.

## The appropriate first obstruction

Schneiderman, *Algebraic linking numbers of knots in 3-manifolds*, Algebraic
& Geometric Topology 3 (2003), 921--968, DOI
[10.2140/agt.2003.3.921](https://doi.org/10.2140/agt.2003.3.921),
[published paper](https://msp.org/agt/2003/3-2/agt-v3-n2-p10-p.pdf),
Section 4.1, printed pp. 934--935, defines the annulus self-intersection
invariant using signed double-point loops in

    Z[ <gamma> \ pi1(W) / <gamma> ] / (g=g^-1, [1]=0),

where gamma is the annulus core image. Proposition 4.1.2 identifies its
vanishing with pairing the singularities by Whitney disks, allowing local
cusps. This does not supply clean embedded framed Whitney disks. Section 4
also tracks indeterminacies when changing singular concordances in a product
3-manifold times I. A nonzero value for one chosen annulus is therefore not
automatically a universal obstruction for its boundary pair. The framing
information discarded by the cusp quotient must be retained separately.

Our next certificate should consequently contain an actual annulus movie,
its whisker and core, every double point's sign and full group word, and the
normal framing. Checking those records is a finite task once the movie exists.
The currently saved group identity does not encode them.

An abelianization sends many different full-group labels to the same deck
exponent. Thus a cancellation in cyclic-cover data cannot be lifted back to
a cancellation of those labels. This is a loss of information, not a measured
nonzero obstruction for the present pair. We have not computed its annulus
self-intersection invariant.

## Why two apparent embedding shortcuts do not apply automatically

Daniel Kasprowski, Mark Powell, Arunima Ray, Peter Teichner, *Embedding surfaces
in 4-manifolds*, arXiv:2201.03961v3, 12 April 2023,
[Theorem 1.2, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.03961), works with topological
4-manifolds. With a good fundamental group and algebraically dual spheres,
vanishing intersection, self-intersection, and Kervaire--Milnor invariants is
equivalent to a relative regular homotopy to an embedding with geometric
duals. Framed algebraic duals remove the extra Kervaire--Milnor obstruction.
This supplies neither unverified dual spheres nor the smooth embedding needed
here. We have not checked the good-group hypothesis for an applicable model.

Danica Kosanovic and Peter Teichner, *A new approach to light bulb tricks:
Disks in 4-manifolds*, arXiv:2209.12015v2, 23 October 2023,
[Theorem 1.5 and Proposition 1.8](https://arxiv.org/html/2209.12015),
do give smooth disk results. Their boundary knot has an embedded dual sphere
in the boundary 3-manifold and is null-homotopic in the 4-manifold. The Norman
trick uses that actual dual sphere. Theorem 1.1 concerns isotopy between
already embedded disks, with a relative Dax invariant. These are not arbitrary
annulus existence statements.

All three primary texts were checked on 18 September 2026, with a separate
Luna literature pass on the latter two. The versions above, rather than
unverified publication metadata, identify exactly what was read.

## Concrete division of work

The small-factor capped-clasper task remains a useful independent diagrammatic
audit of research/42. For progress toward a counterexample, the higher-value
task is now a marked product-disk model and an immersed annulus movie, followed
by the signed group-label calculation. If the first obstruction cancels, the
next task is to construct and inspect the actual Whitney disks; it is not to
launch a larger knot search.

The original desired result remains a smooth standard-B4 slice disk together
with a rigorous nonribbon certificate for its actual boundary. None of the
embedding theorems above identifies that boundary with the Abe--Tagami knot.
