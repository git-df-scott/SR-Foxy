## Claims to stop making

1. "Everyone expects slice-ribbon to be false." No source supports it. GST's "probably false" is about **Generalized Property R**. Hendricks (Quanta 2023): "legitimate controversy".
2. "Slice-ribbon is known false for links." No source found; treat as open.
3. "Every slice disk becomes ribbon after one stabilization." No such theorem; stabilization distance can be arbitrarily large.
4. "Search failure is evidence." Calibrated against GHMR/DG: it is not.
