# Strategy: stop improving the weapon, change the target

Written 2026-09-11 after the first execution round. This supersedes the "where we go
next" section of `FABLE-LOG-1.md` as the campaign's primary direction.

## The diagnosis

Every candidate on the board is immune to every obstruction in existence, and for
one shared structural reason.

Each route-A generator builds its candidate's slice disk out of the exterior of a
**ribbon** disk of a partner knot:

| generator | how the disk arises | consequence |
|---|---|---|
| r = 0 RBG / trace embedding | S^4 = E(D) u (-X(K_1)) = E(D) u (-X(K_2)), with D the partner's *ribbon* disk | exterior has no 3-handles: handle-ribbon |
| Oliveira-Smith on 18nh00000601 | trace embedding from the ribbon disk of K_B | proved handle-ribbon (their Thm 1.2) |
| GST band sums | belt sphere in a no-3-handle handle diagram of B^4 | handle-ribbon-shaped by construction |
| annulus twists (Abe-Tange) | W(A_j(J)) = B^4 with J ribbon | same |

So every candidate is handle-ribbon, hence homotopy-ribbon. And every classical
obstruction - Casson-Gordon extension, metabelian and twisted-Alexander
restrictions, the irregular-dihedral Xi invariant, Park-Powell, Turaev's Theorem H -
is an obstruction to *homotopy*-ribbonness. They cannot fire. That is not bad luck;
it is the architecture of the constructions. Improving the obstructions cannot help.

## The escape

There is exactly one generator of certified-slice knots whose output is **not**
automatically homotopy-ribbon: the Teichner sum.

> **Teichner's lemma** (as cited in Dunfield-Gong section 1.17, [Tei, Lemma 2.5]).
> A knot K is smoothly slice **iff** there is a ribbon knot J with K # J ribbon.

Why the output escapes the trap. Suppose K # J is ribbon with J ribbon. Then
R = J # (-J) is ribbon, K is concordant to K # R (using a slice disk for R), and
K # R is ribbon. The slice disk one builds for K is the concordance from K to K # R
glued to a ribbon disk for K # R. That concordance runs a ribbon disk *backwards*,
so it carries local maxima and its exterior needs 3-handles.

A tempting shortcut fails, and it is worth recording why. One might try: K -> K # R
is a ribbon concordance (birth unknots, fuse them into R, fuse R into K), and K # R
is ribbon, so compose and conclude K is ribbon. That would prove the Slice-Ribbon
Conjecture outright, so it must be wrong, and it is: the composition gives
K <= K # R and U <= K # R in Gordon's order. Both sit *below* K # R. The order says
nothing about their relation, and in particular does not give U <= K.

**So a Teichner-certified slice knot is not known to be ribbon, not known to be
handle-ribbon, and not known to be homotopy-ribbon.** Against such a knot the entire
battery catalogued as dead in `research/02_ribbon_only_obstructions.md` is live
again.

## Correction to ERRATA entry E1

E1 closed the Turaev door. That closure is correct but too broad, and the
distinction matters:

- **Theorem H is dead against handle-ribbon targets.** Its proof uses only
  pi_1-surjectivity, which every handle-ribbon disk supplies. Unchanged.
- **Theorem I is a live route-B family, and it was never tested.** Theorem I
  constructs explicit genus-3 surfaces A(p,q,r,s) whose boundary knots have
  NON-metabolic nil-form when r != 0 and s != 0. By H(ii) those knots are not
  ribbon, and since the proof runs through pi_1-surjectivity they are not
  **homotopy-ribbon**. They are algebraically slice. Theorem J says H gives no
  sliceness obstruction, so the obstruction does not rule out their being slice.

**If any Turaev Theorem I knot is smoothly slice, it is a counterexample** - and a
stronger one than required, separating slice from homotopy-ribbon rather than merely
from ribbon. The paper is essentially uncited and no one appears to have checked.

## The three programs, ranked

**P1. Turaev's Theorem I family (route B, running).** Recover A(p,q,r,s) and its
Seifert matrix from the 1983 text; compute Alexander polynomial, Fox-Milnor,
determinant-square, Arf and Levine-Tristram signatures over a parameter grid; keep
the members with r, s both nonzero that survive every classical sliceness
obstruction. Those are algebraically slice, provably not homotopy-ribbon, and
unobstructed. Then attack sliceness directly. This is the only route where an
existing theorem could produce a proof with no new mathematics.

**P2. Teichner sums past the census (route A).** Generate K # J certificates at 20
to 21 crossings, beyond Dunfield-Gong's 19-crossing census, then run the
*homotopy-ribbon* battery on each survivor. DG built 513 such certificates and all
turned out ribbon, but they established that by searching for disks, not by running
obstructions. The obstruction pass on fresh output is new work. A Teichner-certified
knot failing a homotopy-ribbon obstruction is a counterexample.

**P3. The fusing-band generator**, which unblocks the Abe-Tagami lane. Diagnosed in
`results/concordance_search_diagnosis.md`.

## Honest odds

P1 is the sharpest target we have ever had: small, explicit, with a stronger
certificate than any other lane, and untested for forty years. It is still most
likely that Turaev's knots are simply not slice, because most knots are not, and
Levine-Tristram signatures may well kill the whole grid on the first pass. That
would itself be worth knowing and takes hours rather than months.
