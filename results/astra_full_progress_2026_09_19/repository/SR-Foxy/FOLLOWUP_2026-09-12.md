## Mathematical change in strategy

The new full-ring calculation is more informative than another list of vanishing invariants. It explains why ordinary local-equivalence tests cannot separate these two stored complexes even when the omitted mixed arrows are restored. The lifting argument, exact projection formula, independent checks, and limits are in [the full-ring audit](research/09_full_ring_floer_audit.md). This is an algebraic result conditional on the calculator's correct knot input, not a new claimed theorem about slice disks.

The ribbon chain-retraction condition comes from Ian Zemke, *Knot Floer homology obstructs ribbon concordance*, arXiv:1902.04050 (2019), Section 4, equation (14). The same map has the required hat reduction; tensoring the two-variable homotopy identity with the quotient ring preserves it. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/1902.04050).

Two bounded Opus critiques challenged the grading, lifting, and proof assumptions. Their complete prompts/responses and reported aggregate cost (about $1.19) are preserved. They are advisory model output. The write-up explicitly corrects the second critique's confusion between K1 and the difference D, and its overstatement of what an Alexander-polynomial check validates.
