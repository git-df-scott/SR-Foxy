# Three strikes, 2026-09-12

## Strike 1: the Hom-Park knot, built for the first time

Hom-Park (arXiv:2507.20455) Corollary 1.3 gives explicit knots that are
**algebraically slice and provably not ribbon**:

    P(K,J,p,q1,q2) = K_{p,q1} # -K_{p,q2} # J_{p,q2} # -J_{p,q1}

This campaign has tracked that lane since Session 0 but had never built the knot.
Built here at the smallest instance the paper names, K = T(2,3), J = T(2,5), p = 2,
q1 = 1, q2 = 3, using a Seifert-framed cabling routine written and calibrated for the
purpose (`scripts/cable.py`: the blackboard p-parallel of a braid closure has framing
equal to the writhe, so the Seifert-framed cable needs q - p*w corrective half
twists). Calibration: the routine reproduces genus = p * genus(K) on 3_1, 4_1 and
10_17, and independently rebuilds (10_17)_{2,1} at 41 crossings, matching the
earlier construction.

Result: **104 crossings, one component, signature 0.** Saved as
`data/knots/HomPark_P.json`. HFK is out of reach at that size. The Casson-Gordon
obstruction is running; it had not returned after 20 minutes, which for this routine
means only that no certifying character has been found yet in the searched grid.

Why it matters: this is a second route-B object with a **proved** non-ribbon
certificate and open sliceness, independent of the Miyazaki machinery. If the
obstruction returns nothing, it joins the Abe-Tagami sums on the live board.

## Strike 2: consistency check against Miyazaki (inconclusive, box too large)

Miyazaki's theorem proves D_{0,1} is not ribbon, so a ribbon search on it **must**
fail; success would mean our reconstruction of the Abe-Tagami family is wrong. Run at
2 bands and band length 6, it exceeded the time budget without completing, so the
check is inconclusive rather than passed. Worth rerunning in a box small enough to
terminate.

## Strike 3: can anything separate the Abe-Tagami knots?

The whole lane turns on whether [K_n] = [K_m]. Any concordance invariant that
separates a pair kills it. Levine-Tristram signatures are concordance invariants, so
they are the cheapest available separator.

Computed from the Seifert matrices at roots of unity of order 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9,
11 and 13:

| knot | signatures |
|---|---|
| K_0 = 6_3 | all zero |
| K_1 | all zero |
| K_2 | all zero |

**Identical, and identically zero.** No separation. Combined with tau, epsilon, nu,
the ordinary signature, the branched cover homology and now Casson-Gordon all
agreeing or vanishing across the family, there is no longer any cheap invariant left
to try on this lane. That is not a proof that the knots are concordant, but it is the
reason the lane has stayed open since 2015 and it is now measured rather than
assumed.
