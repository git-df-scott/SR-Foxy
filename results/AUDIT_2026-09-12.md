## Stale jobs stopped

The Teichner run on the r = 0 knots and the GST 2-band search had each burned over
three CPU-hours with no output and were killed. Partial results are kept. The reason
the Teichner run was so slow is diagnostic rather than accidental: the r = 0 knots
have Alexander polynomial 1, so the Fox-Milnor filter rejects nothing and every
banded link reaches the expensive isometry stage. Those knots are the worst possible
input for that search.

## A constraint that directs the next search

Turaev's Theorem I is stated for **K # L**, for any L whose Alexander polynomial is
coprime to that of K. So the nil-form of K # L is non-metabolic whenever K's is, and
therefore:

> **For a Turaev Theorem I knot K, no connected sum K # L with Delta_L coprime to
> Delta_K is ever ribbon.**

That has a direct consequence for the Teichner route. Teichner's lemma says K is
slice iff some ribbon J makes K # J ribbon. Combining, **any Teichner partner J for a
Turaev knot must have Alexander polynomial sharing a factor with
Delta_K = rho(t) rho(1/t)** — that is, divisible by rho or rho*. Searching over
generic small ribbon knots is therefore provably futile, and the search should be
restricted to ribbon knots whose Alexander polynomial carries rho or rho* as a
factor.

This is a real narrowing, and it cuts the other way too: it explains why nobody
stumbles onto such a sum by accident.
