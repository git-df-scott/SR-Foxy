# HY/Fox historical correction

Fox, *Characterization of slices and ribbons*, Osaka J. Math. 10 (1973),
69--76, [primary PDF](https://www.i-repository.net/contents/osakacu/sugaku/111F0000002-01001-8.pdf),
footnote 1 on p. 69, explicitly says Hosokawa--Yanagawa's proof has an error
in the second paragraph of p. 380, communicated by the authors, and refers to
their diagram 2.  The original paper is Hosokawa--Yanagawa, *Is every slice
knot a ribbon knot?*, Osaka J. Math. 2 (1965), 373--384,
[primary PDF](https://ir.library.osaka-u.ac.jp/repo/ouka/all/8798/ojm02_02_10.pdf).

The upstream “no published refutation” diagnosis is therefore false.  HY's
Appendix (pp. 383--384) continues after repeated Dehn-lemma applications: it
chooses a 3-cell and ambient homeomorphisms to make the replacement disks
mutually disjoint while preserving boundary collars.  This supports the
historical correction.  Applicability still requires HY's stated normal,
canonical, properly collared disks with pairwise disjoint boundary curves; the
appendix alone does not establish those hypotheses for the present geometry.
