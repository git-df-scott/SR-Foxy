# Correction register and evidence boundaries

These are handoff warnings based on the preserved reports and chat, not a new referee certification. Earlier records remain unchanged in the archive.

1. **Counterexample status:** no smooth standard-B4 disk for D01 and no global nonribbon proof for KDG were supplied. A candidate collar-map group identity is not an annulus or a disk.
2. **Miyazaki stabilization claims:** audit hypotheses on every prime summand. The old blanket exclusion of prime fibered ribbon partners was retracted. Do not turn its correction into blanket permission or exclusion for all composite partners.
3. **Completed ordinary invariants:** ordinary s for the relevant differences follows from the recorded summand computations and additivity. HKL(2,13) null results are inconclusive. Invalid HFK results remain unknown. Historical resource failures are not zero invariants.
4. **Jones tests:** exact KDG p=3 quotient 6601/nullity 2 and p=4 quotient 106081/nullity 3 pass the tested conditions. D01 p=2 quotient -47/nullity 1 and p=3 quotient -27911/nullity 2 are complete, including the later direct p=3 computation. Old documents describing p=3 as only derived are superseded by the centered-proof repository checkpoint. Passing tests are not positive evidence of concordance.
5. **Transfer proof:** the original all-degree draft's L_n(r^n) formula is false; the repaired statement uses L_n((r+1)^n). Use repository `pass04_0515_centered_proof_and_D01/CORRECTED_TRANSFER_THEOREM.md`. Preserve the original as an explicitly superseded draft. Internal checks are not external peer review; scope is two numerical Eisermann necessities, not all quantum invariants or arbitrary slice links.
6. **Fixed-axis results:** the original nonconjugacy result and its local-knotting retraction apply only with their marked-loop/exterior hypotheses. They do not exclude arbitrary nonlocal disk changes, new axes, or all stabilizations. Regenerating the marked presentation is not identical to proving the diagram is the cited paper's exact marked figure.
7. **Crossed-band genus:** two annuli joined by the specified two boundary bands give genus one with two boundary components, not an annulus. An actual framed nonseparating compression disk or separately embedded annulus remains needed.
8. **Relative Reidemeister move:** pass06's first bigon-blockage assertion was corrected in pass07: a permitted RIII then RII realizes the first cancellation while preserving R. The 6478-state RIII search of depth at most four is bounded evidence only.
9. **Whisker branches:** 49 fixed-meridian boundary colorings, including 36 witness/witness ones split 24 separating/12 nonseparating, do not identify which representations extend over the actual product-disk exterior. Counts are not probabilities. Pass09 supplies a particular algebraically certified map with full-group equality, but not its geometric collar realization.
10. **Framing shortcuts:** the diag(2,-2) failure and its sign variants exclude only inherited framed classes with the other handles omitted. Passing an integral determinant test does not identify S3 or a standard B4. A full mixed hyperbolic lattice basis is not a handle cancellation certificate.
11. **Theorem A/A-prime:** do not summarize every twist parity as one smooth S2xS2 stabilization. The recorded general statement distinguishes parity and its smooth k=1 argument from a general homeomorphism-level conclusion. Determinant -1 alone does not prove the boundary is S3.
12. **isoSig:** equal full signatures in compatible conventions give a combinatorial triangulation identification; different signatures need not imply different manifolds. Random simplification can miss a match, not invalidate an exact equal signature. Old unpinned slopes remain unusable without their original marked input. Orientation/peripheral marking requires separate data.
13. **Slope bookkeeping:** independent signs +/- on an unoriented filling slope are duplicates. The 65536 signed tuples reduce to 4096 before other symmetries; 96 signed two-and-two assignments reduce to six. Do not count 59 reported hits as 59 distinct surgeries. A finite coordinate box is not guaranteed to contain the true lifted slopes after basis changes.
14. **Positive identifications:** numerical volume agreement is not an independent proof of hyperbolicity. A stored positive isometry result and an explicit orientation/peripheral certificate are different deliverables. Exceptions silently skipped by the old homology filter were not valid exclusions. Which lifts form a deck pair must come from the covering map, not equal-looking coordinates.
15. **Different four-manifolds:** arbitrary fillings giving the right boundary do not identify the actual branched trace. A framed surgery matrix is not all the link Floer data required for a generic d-invariant computation.
16. **Donaldson:** D01 slice supplies a concordance and a branched rational homology cobordism between Y0 and Y1; it does not make Y1 rationally null-cobordant. Specify caps, orientations, fundamental-group/theorem hypotheses, total rank and target lattice before calling a nonembedding an obstruction.
17. **Trace-cap exclusion:** the merged Opus writeup accepts the degree-two pullback 2H argument, so this particular actual branched-trace-plus-cap cannot be negative definite. This is not a ban on all definite fillings or all Donaldson/Floer methods. Amphichirality of L(13,5) does not by itself supply missing oriented markings for the target or for a prescribed trace.
18. **Agol-Ren material:** the newest merged PR includes a reformulation and an explicitly unverified Alexander-divisibility/minimality step. This archiving pass did not independently check those source hypotheses. A reformulation as another open question is not progress in constructing a certificate, and is not a reason to declare the search impossible.
19. **Runtime and publication claims:** script execution and publication claims inside historic files apply to their original turns. Some attachments were originally local-only, some were superseded by later repository checkpoints. The present manifest verifies preservation, not all historic mathematical claims. Do not claim this archive has been pushed before remote hash verification succeeds.
