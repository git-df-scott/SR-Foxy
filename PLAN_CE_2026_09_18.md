## 0. The target, in its sharpest known form

A CE is a knot smoothly slice in the **standard** `B^4` and globally non-ribbon.
Three published statements say the same thing from different angles, and together
they pin the shape of any CE:

* **Agol-Ren Cor 1.14(1)** (their attribution: Miyazaki; see [Bak16, Rmk 6]).
  If slice-ribbon holds, every concordance class contains **at most one** fibered
  knot minimal for `<=_h`. So:
  > **A CE is exactly a concordance class containing two distinct `<=_h`-minimal
  > fibered knots.**
* **Baker**, *A note on the concordance of fibered knots*, arXiv:1409.7646,
  J. Topol. 9 (2016) 1-4, abstract verbatim: *"Either fibered knots supporting the
  tight contact structure are unique in their smooth concordance class or there
  exists a fibered counterexample to the Slice-Ribbon Conjecture."*
* **Abe-Tagami**, arXiv:2210.04044, abstract verbatim: *"we prove that all tight
  fibered knots are minimal in this partially ordered set."*

The third makes the first two the same statement: **tight fibered knots are
`<=_h`-minimal for free**, so two distinct concordant tight fibered knots *is* a
CE, with no minimality argument needed. That is the cleanest target available.

**A minimality criterion that needs no tightness** (from Corollary M,
`results/opus_2026_09_18_0005_hr_predecessors_are_fibered/`): if `Delta_J` is
irreducible of degree `2g(J)` and `J` is not slice, then `J` is `<=_h`-minimal.
Proof: Theorem F makes every predecessor fibered; the genus lemma drops the genus;
Friedl-Powell forces `Delta | Delta_J`, so `Delta = 1`, so the predecessor is `U`,
so `J` is slice.

## 1. The structural law that kills most ideas

> **Fox-Milnor and Miyazaki's second alternative are mutually exclusive.**
> Alternative 2 says no `f` has `f(t)f(1/t) | Delta_K`. Fox-Milnor says a slice
> `K` has `Delta_K = f(t)f(1/t)`. So a knot satisfying alternative 2 with
> `Delta != 1` is **not slice**, and its non-ribbon certificate is useless.

This is why every CE candidate must be a **connected sum of at least two** prime
fibered summands: the alternatives are tested on the **summands**, Fox-Milnor on
the **sum**. `D_{0,1} = K_0 # (-K_1)` works because each summand carries the
irreducible `Delta` while the sum carries the norm `Delta^2`.

It also kills, in one line each:

| idea | why it dies |
|---|---|
| a single prime fibered knot with irreducible `Delta` | not slice, by Fox-Milnor |
| `K # R` with `R` ribbon | not prime |
| satellites of concordant knots | `Delta_P(t) Delta_K(t^w)`, generically reducible |
| winding-number-0 satellites of slice knots | fix `Delta` and primality, but **are not fibered**: the fibration class restricted to the companion solid torus is `w = 0`, so that piece cannot fiber |
| the `Delta_r` boundary family | `Delta_r = f_r f_r^*` with `f_r` not symmetric, so it never splits into two **symmetric** summands: Proposition N |

### The HP lane is the sharpest specification

Hom-Park arXiv:2507.20455: a connected sum of `gamma_0`-sharp fibered knots is
ribbon **iff** of the form `K # -K`; Cor 1.3 gives, for distinct iterated cables
`K, J` of tight fibered knots and `q_1 != q_2` coprime to `p`,

```
P = K_{p,q1} # -K_{p,q2} # J_{p,q2} # -J_{p,q1}
```

**algebraically slice and not ribbon**. So

> **CE `<=>` the 4-term relation `[K_{p,q1}] - [K_{p,q2}] - [J_{p,q1}] + [J_{p,q2}] = 0`
> holds in the concordance group for some distinct iterated cables of tight
> fibered knots.**

Smallest instance: `K = T(2,3)`, `J = T(2,5)`, `p = 2`, `q_1 = 1`, `q_2 = 3`.
`OBSTRUCTION_MATRIX.md` marks nearly every HP cell NOT YET COMPUTED. One is filled
here, by Hom's cabling formula `tau(K_{p,q}) = p tau(K) + (p-1)(q-1)/2` (valid
since torus knots have `epsilon = 1`):

```
tau = 2, 3, 4, 5  for  T(2,3)_{2,1}, T(2,3)_{2,3}, T(2,5)_{2,1}, T(2,5)_{2,3}
tau(P) = 2 - 3 + 5 - 4 = 0
```

`tau` does not obstruct, as Cor 1.3's "algebraically slice" already implies.

**But filling the rest of those cells is predicted to be worthless.**
Dai-Hom-Stoffregen-Truong arXiv:1806.06225 exhibit cable families whose
independence **cannot be detected by any combination of algebraic concordance
invariants, Casson-Gordon invariants, `tau`, `epsilon` and `Upsilon`**. So zeros
in those cells are forced, not evidence — the same trap as Lemma B of
`research/33`. **Do not run them and report survival.**

## 3. What is actually missing — ranked

**M1. A ribbon-only obstruction.** Nothing in the literature separates ribbon from
handle-ribbon. The only computable ribbon-only family, Eisermann's link theorems,
is vacuous for knots (`n = 1`) and for 2-component slice links
(`research/22` §3.1). This single missing tool would settle KDG immediately, and
it is a genuine open research problem, not a computation.

**M2. A construction that produces slice disks on non-ribbon-looking knots.** The
only known machine is exotic-4-manifold standardisation — precisely how KDG was
born (Dunfield-Gong sphere, standardised by Oliveira-Smith). The AT lane has no
second construction; its one positive machine, the annulus-twist trace, provably
lands one `S^2 x S^2` away and cannot be destabilised.

**M3. A method to decide the 4-term cable relation.** Blocked by DHST above.

**M4. Two distinct concordant tight fibered knots.** Would be a CE outright, with
minimality free. No example is known; Baker's dichotomy has stood since 2014.

**M5. Decidability of the 0-surgery pairing.** My census found 1589 volume-matched
0-surgery groups among prime fibered knots with irreducible `Delta`, robust from
`1e-6` to `1e-10`. SnapPea's `is_isometric_to` refuses on closed manifolds;
`isometry_signature` works (canonical retriangulation, so **not** the retracted
`isoSig` method) at ~6 s/knot for 3486 knots. **Unfinished, resumable, cheapest
open item.**
