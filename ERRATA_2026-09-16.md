# Errata, 16 September 2026: the fibered-partner exclusion is withdrawn

This file follows the convention of `ERRATA_2026-09-11.md`: the corrections below
are **binding on every other file in this repository**, and where another file
still says the old thing, this file wins.

One correction, and it changes what is on the board.

## E16-1 — Miyazaki Thm 5.5 was restated without a hypothesis, and three searches were cancelled on the strength of it

**Old claim.** Stated in five places, in substantially identical words:

> `8_9`, `8_20`, `9_27` must **not** be run: they are fibered, and adding a
> fibered ribbon `J` leaves `K_0` and `-K_1` unpaired among the fibered prime
> summands, so Miyazaki still gives non-ribbon and no certificate can exist.

Sites: `UNFINISHED.md` §2; `HANDOFF.md` ("Where the Teichner lane ends");
`SESSION_2026-09-15.md` ("Where the Teichner lane ends"); `research/24` §3; and
the module docstring of `scripts/teichner_mirror_partners.py`, where the
exclusion is also baked into the default `PARTNERS` list.

**Correction.** The restatement drops a hypothesis, and with the hypothesis
restored the theorem does not apply. Miyazaki's Theorem 5.5 reads, verbatim from
Abe–Tagami [S06] Appendix A:

> For `i = 1, ..., n`, let `K_i` be a prime fibered knot in a homotopy 3-sphere
> `M_i` **satisfying one of the following**:
> * `K_i` is minimal with respect to `≥` among all fibered knots in homology
>   spheres,
> * there is no `f(t) ∈ Z[t] \ {±t^k}` such that `f(t)f(t^{-1}) | Δ_{K_i}(t)`.
>
> If `K_1 # ... # K_n` is homotopically ribbon, then the set `{1, ..., n}` can be
> paired into `⊔_{s=1}^{m} {i_s, j_s}` such that `K_{i_s} = conj(K_{j_s})`.

The quantifier is over **every** `i`, and `ABE_TAGAMI_AUDIT.md` already states
this correctly ("minimal in the homotopy-ribbon order **or** satisfy the stated
Alexander-polynomial condition"). The audit is right; the five planning sites are
wrong.

**Why the exclusion collapses.** Let `J` be any **nontrivial ribbon** knot. Then
`J` satisfies *neither* alternative:

* *Not minimal.* `J` ribbon gives a ribbon concordance `U → J`, so `J ≥ U` with
  `U` the unknot, which is a fibered knot in `S^3`. Since `J ≠ U`, `J` is not
  minimal. (Independently: Gordon's Lemma 3.4, as used by Hom–Park, gives
  `g(J) > g(U) = 0`.)
* *Norm factor exists.* Fox–Milnor gives `Δ_J(t) = f(t)f(t^{-1})` up to units.
  `J` nontrivial and fibered forces `deg Δ_J = 2g(J) > 0`, so `f ∉ {±t^k}` and
  `f(t)f(t^{-1}) | Δ_J(t)`. The second alternative fails, with `f` as an explicit
  witness.

So Theorem 5.5 does not apply to `D_{0,1} # J`, and **cannot obstruct the
ribbonness of any Teichner sum**, for any nontrivial ribbon partner, fibered or
not. That is not a quirk of these three partners; it is structural. It is also
exactly what the Teichner lane needs to be true — `K # J` ribbon must not imply
`K` ribbon, or the lane would be vacuous — so the old claim was in tension with
the repository's own reason for running the lane.

**Evidence.** `PRIMARY RESTATEMENT (two independent), VERIFIED`, plus a local
computation.

* Abe–Tagami arXiv:1502.01102 LaTeX source (`arxiv.tex`, Appendix A, `\begin{thm}`
  at line 517), fetched from `arxiv.org/e-print/1502.01102` on 2026-09-16.
  Quantifier and both alternatives as quoted above.
* Hom–Park arXiv:2507.20455 LaTeX source, independently: Theorem 5.5 "states that
  if a connected sum of knots that **are** minimal with respect to homotopy ribbon
  concordance among all fibered knots in homology spheres is ribbon, then it must
  be of the form `K # -K`". Their proof is load-bearing on the quantifier: they
  prove minimality for **every** summand (each `γ_0`-sharp fibered knot) before
  invoking 5.5.
* `scripts/miyazaki_hypothesis_check.py` decides the second alternative
  exhaustively over `Z`, no floating point: `8_9`, `8_20`, `9_27` each **FAIL**
  it with an explicit witness `f`, as do `6_1` and `8_8`. Controls `3_1`, `4_1`
  and `K_n = A_n(6_3)` PASS, as they must — `K_n`'s `Δ = 1 - 3t + 5t² - 3t³ + t⁴`
  is irreducible, which is precisely why Miyazaki *does* apply to `D_{n,m}` and
  the non-ribbon certificate on `D_{0,1}` is untouched.
* Fiberedness of all three confirmed locally, `snappy` 3.3.2
  `knot_floer_homology()`: `8_9` fibered genus 3, `8_20` fibered genus 2, `9_27`
  fibered genus 3. The old claim's premise was correct; its inference was not.

**The AMS original was not obtained.** `ams.org` returns a Cloudflare 403 to this
container, as already recorded for [S29]. Both restatements above are secondary,
though one of them is the restatement this repository has always cited as
[S06, Thm 4.1]. The correction itself does not depend on the bibliography: the
argument that a nontrivial ribbon knot fails both alternatives is a two-line
proof from Fox–Milnor and Gordon, and it would have to be the *alternatives*
themselves that are misquoted for it to fail. Marked `SECONDARY RESTATEMENT ×2`
in `SOURCES.md` pending the original.

**Consequence.**
1. The a-priori exclusion of `8_9`, `8_20`, `9_27` is withdrawn. They are
   legitimate Teichner partners at 33, 33 and 34 crossings — the cheapest
   remaining band, below the 34- and 35-crossing partners the lane was otherwise
   reduced to.
2. `research/24` §4b's "within a handful of runs of exhausting its tractable
   partners" is off by three, in the cheap direction.
3. The correct rule, replacing the wrong one: Miyazaki 5.5 excludes `D_{0,1} # J`
   **iff every prime summand of `J` satisfies one of the two alternatives.** This
   is a real filter and it cuts both ways. A prime fibered ribbon `J` is *never*
   excluded (it fails at itself). But `J = L # (-L)` — the square knot
   `3_1 # (-3_1)` being the smallest case — *is* excluded: its prime summands
   `3_1` and `-3_1` are fibered with irreducible `Δ = t² - t + 1`, which passes
   the second alternative, so the sum `K_0 # (-K_1) # 3_1 # (-3_1)` is inside the
   theorem, `3_1` pairs with `-3_1`, and `K_0` would have to pair with `-K_1`.
   No run on such a `J` can ever produce a certificate. Neither the old rule nor
   its naive negation gets this case right.
4. Running: `results/teichner_D01_fibered_partners.json`, pre-registered at
   `results/PREREG_teichner_fibered_partners_2026-09-16.md`, in the same box as
   the completed `8_8` run.

**What this correction is not.** It is not evidence that a certificate exists. It
removes a wrong reason not to look. The two comparable completed runs (`6_1` at
31 crossings, `8_8` at 33) found nothing, and the stated prior on these three is
low.

## Claims to stop making

Added to the list in `ERRATA_2026-09-11.md`:

5. "Miyazaki's pairing theorem forbids a fibered ribbon partner in a Teichner
   sum." It does not, and it cannot — the partner itself fails both of the
   theorem's alternatives. Any future use of Miyazaki 5.5 must check the
   hypothesis **on every prime summand** and record which alternative each one
   satisfies.
