## What was asked and what was done

The brief was to attack the missing implication on the Abe–Tagami lane — a
smooth concordance `K_0 ≃ K_1`, equivalently a smooth disk for
`D_{0,1} = K_0 # (−K_1)` — starting from the actual annulus-twist geometry, and
not to produce a menu of options. Six results, all verified here:

1. **Theorem A.** The `n = 1` annulus twist has an *integral* trace: `S³ × I`
   plus two 2-handles on the Hopf link `c'_1 ∪ c'_2` with framings `2` and `0`.
   `K_0 × I` is disjoint from both (`lk(K, c'_i) = 0`), so `K_0` and `K_1`
   cobound a smooth annulus in a 4-manifold with even hyperbolic intersection
   form, i.e. in `(S³ × I) # (S²×S²)`. Hence `D_{0,1}` bounds a smooth disk in
   `B⁴ # (S²×S²)`: **at most one stabilization** separates this lane from a
   counterexample, and it is zero precisely in the counterexample case. `n = ±1` is the only annulus twist with integral slopes.
2. **Lemma B.** Same 0-surgery (meridian-preserving) ⟹ Blanchfield isometry
   (Borodzik–Friedl) ⟹ Seifert matrices S-equivalent (Trotter 1973) ⟹ equal
   algebraic concordance class. So every `D_{n,m}` is algebraically slice *by
   theorem*, and no Seifert-form invariant — Fox–Milnor, all Levine–Tristram
   signatures, Milnor signatures, Arf, the Levine class — can ever obstruct it.
   The repository's long record of vanishing classical computations on this
   family was never evidence.
3. **`Σ₂(K_1)` is hyperbolic** (numerically; vol `7.2813263517`, 9 tetrahedra),
   resolving the "unrecognized" status in `research/21` §4. Negatively, this
   removes the Ozsváth–Szabó plumbing algorithm — the only route implementable
   at this scale — from the `d`-invariant gate of `research/21` §3.
4. **The linking-form metabolizer gate is passed at `p = 2, 3, 5`.** At `p = 2`,
   `λ(Σ₂(K_n))(g,g) = 5/13, 6/13, 7/13` for `n = 0,1,2`, all non-residues, so
   every pairwise product is a residue. At `p = 3`, `H_1 = (Z/7)²` with equal
   discriminants. At `p = 5`, `H_1 = (Z/4)⁴` and an explicit metabolizer is
   exhibited and verified exhaustively (all 65,536 pairings). Exact integer and
   rational arithmetic throughout, with lens-space and ribbon-knot controls.
5. **Proposition C.** For `p` an odd prime and `H_1(Σ_p(K))` elementary abelian
   of exponent `q ≡ 1 (mod p)`, the linking form is *always* hyperbolic. So the
   `p = 3` gate was vacuous before it was run. Proved, and corroborated by a
   census of the knot table: 372/372 hyperbolic when `q ≡ 1`, 271/271
   anisotropic when `q ≡ −1`, zero violations.
6. **A ribbon control refuted a tempting shortcut** before it could mislead:
   the Gauss-sum criterion `GS(λ) = +|H_1|^{1/2}` for metabolic linking forms
   is false over even-order groups (`8_20`, ribbon, gives `−8` not `+4`), so it
   is recorded with that restriction and not applied at `p = 5`.

## What this does not do

Theorem A is *not* evidence for `[K_0] = [K_1]`. The same construction at
`n = −1` gives the same form `H` with endpoints `K_0` and `K_{−1} = K_0`. And
`research/14` §3 already proves `π₁(W ∖ C) = Z`, which forbids destabilizing
*this* annulus. Theorem A and that obstruction are two halves of one statement.
